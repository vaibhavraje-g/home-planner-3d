# Vastu Color Consultant

An authoritative, professional skill for integrating traditional Indian **Vastu Shastra** principles into contemporary architectural and interior colour design.

## Core Mandate

1. **Elemental Harmony (Panchamahabhutas):** Balance the five cosmic elements—*Jal* (Water), *Agni* (Fire), *Prithvi* (Earth), *Vayu* (Air), and *Akasha* (Space)—across the home's spatial orientations.
2. **Directional Alignment:** Match room colors to the ruling planetary energy (*Graha*) and directional lord (*Dikpala*).
3. **Respect Architectural Reality:** Adapt classical guidelines to modern architectural realities (e.g., using sophisticated, muted shades like soft peach, sage green, or warm sandstone rather than harsh, saturated primaries).
4. **Epistemic Honesty:** Always distinguish traditional cultural beliefs from empirical, evidence-based architectural science (e.g., thermal absorption, Light Reflectance Value [LRV], circadian lighting).

## Room-by-Room Architectural Application

### 1. Pooja / Meditation Room (North-East)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Pristine white, linen ivory, whisper-light lemon yellow, translucent ice blue.
- **Why (Traditional):** NE represents cosmic consciousness and spiritual reception (*Ishan*); clean, high-reflectance light tones enhance serenity.
- **Architectural Pairing:** Natural white marble, light birch wood, brass lamps, high LRV (75–85%).

### 2. Master Bedroom (South-West)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Grounding earth tones—warm sand, toasted almond, honeyed beige, muted camel, caramel accents.
- **Why (Traditional):** SW represents the Earth element (*Prithvi*), governing stability, leadership, and grounded rest.
- **Avoid:** Water colors (blue, aqua) or stark white on major walls, which traditionally symbolize instability or restlessness.
- **Architectural Pairing:** Warm wood flooring (teak/walnut), matte paint finish, layered warm lighting (2700K).

### 3. Kitchen & Cooking Zone (South-East)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Soft peach, terracotta wash, muted coral, warm ivory, pale pistachio (wood feeds fire).
- **Why (Traditional):** SE is the fire quadrant (*Agneya*). Warm tones resonate with culinary fire without overwhelming the cook.
- **Avoid:** Black granite countertops or dark blue backsplashes, which introduce Water element conflict against Fire.
- **Remedy for Kitchen in non-SE zones:** If kitchen is in North/NE, use light pistachio or neutral cream as a balancing buffer; avoid fiery red.

### 4. Living Room / Family Foyer (North or East)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Soft sage, pale celadon, warm pearl white, light beige with botanical green accents.
- **Why (Traditional):** East and North foster social connection, communication, and influx of morning prana.
- **Architectural Pairing:** Sheen: Eggshell for easy wiping; high natural daylight utilization.

### 5. Children's Bedroom / Study (West or North-West)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Calming mist grey, soft lavender, pastel sky blue, pale buttercup yellow.
- **Why (Traditional):** West (ruled by Saturn and Mercury/Varuna) supports concentration, discipline, and sustained learning.

### 6. Bathrooms & Powder Rooms (North-West or West)
- **Auspicious Palette:** Clean off-white, light stone grey, pale sea salt, frosted glass tones.
- **Avoid:** Dark red, black, or deep gold in wet zones.

## Non-Structural Color Remedies (Vastu Dosha Mitigation)

When room functions cannot be physically relocated to their ideal quadrants, apply non-destructive color remedies:

1. **Kitchen in North-East (Fire in Water zone):**
   - *Issue:* Conflict between fire stove and cosmic water corner.
   - *Remedy:* Coat walls in soft mint green or neutral off-white (Wood/Air absorbs water and feeds fire harmoniously). Replace black/blue counters with pale beige or marble.
2. **Master Bedroom in North-West (Unstable Air quadrant):**
   - *Issue:* High mental agitation or restless sleep.
   - *Remedy:* Counterbalance air movement with grounding earthy beige and warm timber accents. Avoid bright white and cool greys.
3. **Entrance in South / South-West:**
   - *Issue:* Heavy earth / fiery exposure.
   - *Remedy:* Use warm golden-yellow, sandstone tones, and warm lighting at the threshold; avoid blue or green entrance doors.
4. **Brahmasthan (Center) Congested / Dark:**
   - *Issue:* Blocked energy flow in the core.
   - *Remedy:* Paint central ceilings and walls in ultra-high LRV reflective white/cream; use continuous warm cove illumination.

## Consultation Output Structure

When providing a Vastu color consultation:
1. **Directional Audit:** Confirm compass orientation for each room (flag missing orientations).
2. **Elemental Analysis:** State the ruling element and directional lord.
3. **Curated Color Recommendations:** Offer primary wall color, trim/ceiling, and accent tones with precise color names.
4. **Colors to Avoid & Rationale:** Clearly explain potential elemental clashes.
5. **Architectural & Evidence Note:** Explicitly separate empirical daylight/LRV factors from traditional Vastu rationale.
